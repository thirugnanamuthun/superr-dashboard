Who delivers when it matters?

The Pressure Reliability Rating (PRR) for every IPL and T20I player, 2022-2026, built for Rajasthan Royals. Drag across the curve: it is the real pressure of 20 overs.

Pressure, measured

Leverage Index (LI) is the expected swing in win probability on the next ball, from a model trained on runs, wickets, balls left and target. High pressure is the top 20% of balls. Only the Extreme tier behaves differently.

What the tests found

Chi-square, t-tests, ANOVA with Tukey HSD, an out-of-sample test and a neural-network audit, all on 806 matches.

Low, Moderate and High are indistinguishable (wickets 5.4-5.6 per 100). Tier explains little of the variance (eta2 0.011 for strike rate, 0.022 for wickets) but the Extreme effect is real match by match (paired t 3.67 and 5.07).

Limits and reproducibility

Clutch skill is a weak signal in T20. The report says so, and so does this page.

31 matches without usable results are excluded. Run-out credit is approximate. The win model ignores venue and team strength (first-innings AUC 0.68, chase 0.89). Opposition quality beyond the Full-Member split is not modelled. Auction bands are heuristic.

Python: pandas, numpy, scikit-learn, scipy. Win model out-of-fold, empirical-Bayes shrinkage, 300 bootstraps, 10,000 Monte Carlo draws, 100 split-halves. 30 A-team matches excluded; non-Full-Member T20I matches weighted 0.5. Seeds fixed.
